Overview

Architecture Notes is a practical field guide built from sixteen years of designing and running enterprise platforms for global banks. Each post explains one pattern the way an architect would: the problem it solves, how it works, a diagram, working code, the trade-offs, and the mistakes that show up in production.

The first series, the Microservices Patterns Field Guide, covers {{ site.posts.size }} patterns across {{ live_categories }} categories — from sagas and transactional outboxes to circuit breakers, service meshes and distributed tracing.

Written by Karthik Sukumaran, AI & GenAI Solutions Architect.
