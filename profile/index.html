Selected case studies

Outcomes, not just architecture diagrams.

GenAI · Agentic

HCLTech AIForce D2C

Designed & built · HCLTech · 2021 – present

3 days → 1 hour average design-to-code effort

Challenge

Turning Figma designs into production-ready front-end and CMS code was slow, manual work — around three days per design on average.

Approach

An agentic design-to-code platform: multi-model LLM orchestration (OpenAI, Claude), RAG over ChromaDB and Pinecone, and autonomous agents that interpret UI, generate code and refine it through feedback loops. Containerised on Azure, with a companion plugin published to the Figma Community.

Result

Production-ready React, Angular, Next.js and CMS output in about an hour, held to enterprise standards for performance, accessibility and maintainability.

Modernisation · Banking

Citibank APAC content platform

Manager · Citibank N.A. · 2018 – 2021

9 countries · zero SAST/DAST findings

Challenge

A legacy SOAP-based content management platform serving Australia, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, the Philippines and Indonesia, in a highly regulated environment.

Approach

Led the re-architecture into microservices with one shared business layer serving both legacy SOAP consumers and new REST APIs; embedded secure-coding standards and code reviews; upgraded the full stack (RHEL, WAS, IHS, database, Documentum, Captiva) in every market; ran cross-team DR drills.

Result

A codebase with zero SAST and DAST findings, a platform upgraded across all nine markets, and maintenance delivered within the change windows set by business and compliance.

My approach to enterprise AI

Four principles behind every platform I design.

Shaped by years in banking, where reliability, auditability and security are non-negotiable.

  1. Grounded in your data

    RAG over governed enterprise sources, so answers are anchored in the organisation's own context.

  2. Model-agnostic

    Multi-model orchestration and routing across providers such as OpenAI and Claude — no single-vendor lock-in.

  3. Agentic where it pays

    Autonomous agents with iterative feedback loops (LangGraph, MCP) for work that benefits from planning and self-correction.

  4. Governed by default

    Usage governance, guardrails and evaluation designed in, with the observability operations teams expect.
